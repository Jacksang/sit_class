# MIE2001 — Assessment Rubrics (10-Week)

---

## 1. Weekly Reflections (20%)

**Format:** 150 words, submitted before each class (Weeks 1-9)

| Criteria | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Satisfactory (2) | Needs Work (1) |
|----------|---------------|----------|------------------|----------------|
| Depth | Connects to personal journey | Links to course concepts | Summarizes without insight | Superficial |
| Application | Applies concept to own idea | One application example | Mentions concept without application | No attempt |
| Breadth | Multiple perspectives | Awareness of alternatives | Single perspective | No analysis |

---

## 2. Customer Discovery Report (25%)

**Format:** 3-4 pages, group submission. Due Week 5.

| Criteria | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Satisfactory (2) | Needs Work (1) |
|----------|---------------|----------|------------------|----------------|
| Problem clarity | Specific, evidence-backed | Clear, some evidence | Vague, little evidence | No clear problem |
| Interview quality | 5+ target-user, open-ended, rich | 3-4, reasonably good | 1-2, leading questions | No interviews |
| Learning | Shows interviews changed understanding | Describes learning | Lists without insight | No learning |
| Market sizing | TAM/SAM/SOM calculated | Two tiers | One tier | None |
| Competitive analysis | Direct + indirect + status quo | Two of three | One type | None |
| Structure | Well-organized, compelling | Clear structure | Some organization | Disorganized |

---

## 3. Business Model Canvas (25%)

**Format:** 1-page BMC + 2-page justification. Due Week 9.

| Criteria | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Satisfactory (2) | Needs Work (1) |
|----------|---------------|----------|------------------|----------------|
| Completeness | All 9 blocks filled, specific | 8-9 blocks, minor gaps | 5-7 blocks | <5 blocks |
| Coherence | One consistent story | Most align | Some contradictions | Don't fit |
| Evidence | Each block justified by research | Most justified | Unsupported | No research basis |
| Iteration | Clear evolution from draft | Some changes | Minor changes | No iteration |

---

## 4. Final Business Plan (30%)

**Format:** Max 6 pages, group submission. Take-home, Week 10.

| Criteria | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Satisfactory (2) | Needs Work (1) |
|----------|---------------|----------|------------------|----------------|
| Executive Summary | Clear, compelling, 3-4 sentences | Clear summary | Vague | Missing |
| Problem & Discovery | Specific problem, strong interview evidence | Good problem, some evidence | Vague, limited | No clear problem |
| Solution & MVP | MVP tested, results shown | MVP designed | Theoretical | No MVP |
| BMC | Complete, coherent, evidence-backed | Mostly complete | Partial | Incomplete |
| Financials | Realistic projection, healthy unit econ | Reasonable projection | Minimal | Missing |
| Overall quality | Well-organized, professional | Clear structure | Some organization | Disorganized |

---

## 5. Participation & Peer Evaluation (Ongoing)

| Criteria | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Satisfactory (2) | Needs Work (1) |
|----------|---------------|----------|------------------|----------------|
| Class participation | Meaningful every session | Most sessions | Occasionally | Rarely |
| Peer feedback | Specific, constructive, actionable | Good feedback | Vague | Negative/absent |
| Team contribution | "Would work with them again" | "Good team member" | "Did their share" | "Didn't contribute" |

---

## Grading Philosophy
**70% Process, 30% Outcome** — We grade the journey. A failed idea with deep learning > successful idea with no learning.

**What "A" work looks like:**
- 10+ customer interviews proving the idea wrong
- Iterated BMC based on real feedback
- MVP built and tested
- Clear, honest presentation
- Strong team and class contribution

**What "C" work looks like:**
- 2-3 interviews, BMC completed but not iterated
- MVP theoretical
- Adequate but uninspired
- Inconsistent participation
