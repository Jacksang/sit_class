# MIE2001 — Course Analysis (10-Week Compressed)

**Target:** First-year BDS, Singapore Institute of Technology
**Duration:** 10 weeks
**Prerequisites:** None

---

## 10-Week Curriculum Map

| Week | Topic | Activity | Deliverable |
|------|-------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | Entrepreneurship Mindset | "Pitch a Bad Idea" | Reflection |
| 2 | Effectuation Principles ⭐ | "My Effectual Profile" | Reflection |
| 3 | Design Thinking & Problem-Finding | Mini design sprint | HMW statements, Team formed |
| 4 | Opportunity Recognition & Customer Discovery | Campus scavenger hunt + interview prep | Interview guide |
| 5 | Opportunity Assessment & Market Sizing | Market sizing workshop | **Customer Discovery Report** |
| 6 | Lean Startup, MVP & Midterm Check | MVP Design Sprint + informal pitch | MVP test plan |
| 7 | Startup Team & Choosing Customers | Team charter + channel brainstorm | Team charter + persona |
| 8 | Project Finance & Singapore Funding | Financial model workshop | Financial model |
| 9 | Business Model Canvas & Pitching | BMC gallery walk + pitch practice | **BMC due** + pitch deck |
| 10 | **Final Business Plan** (Take-home) | No class | Business Plan + Peer eval |

---

## Assessment Structure

| Component | Weight | Due |
|-----------|--------|-----|
| Weekly Reflections | 20% | Weeks 1-9 |
| Customer Discovery Report | 25% | Week 5 |
| Business Model Canvas | 25% | Week 9 |
| Final Business Plan | 30% | Week 10 |

## Core Concepts by Week

### Week 1: Mindset
- What is entrepreneurship? Myths vs. reality
- Growth mindset (Dweck)
- Intro to effectuation vs. causation

### Week 2: Effectuation ⭐
- 5 principles deep dive
- Singapore cases: Carousell, Grab
- Causation vs. effectuation

### Week 3: Design Thinking
- Stanford d.school 5-stage model
- Problem-framing (HMW statements)

### Week 4: Opportunity Recognition
- Sources of opportunity
- Problem-first approach
- Customer discovery & The Mom Test

### Week 5: Opportunity Assessment
- Market sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM)
- Competitive analysis
- Feasibility framework

### Week 6: Lean Startup & MVP
- Build-Measure-Learn
- MVP types
- Riskiest assumption testing

### Week 7: Team & Customers
- Co-founder dynamics, equity, team charter
- Customer segmentation, personas, channels

### Week 8: Project Finance
- Revenue models
- Unit economics (CAC, LTV)
- Break-even
- Singapore funding landscape

### Week 9: BMC & Pitching
- All 9 BMC blocks
- Pitch deck structure
- Storytelling
- Q&A handling

### Week 10: Final (Take-home)
- Business Plan submission
- Peer evaluation
