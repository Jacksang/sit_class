## Part 2: Business Model Canvas (40 min)

### Slide 4 — The Full BMC
```
┌──────────┬──────────┬──────────┐
│ Partners │ Activities│ Value   │
│          ├──────────┤ Prop    │
│          │Resources │         │
├──────────┴──────────┴──────────┤
│ Cost Structure │ Revenue Streams│
└────────────────┴───────────────┘
```

### Slide 5 — Right Side (Customer-Facing)
1. Customer Segments — who?
2. Value Propositions — what value?
3. Channels — how to reach?
4. Customer Relationships — what relationship?
5. Revenue Streams — how they pay?

### Slide 6 — Left Side (Business-Facing)
6. Key Resources — what do we need?
7. Key Activities — what do we do?
8. Key Partnerships — who helps us?
9. Cost Structure — what does it cost?

### Slide 7 — Value Proposition Canvas
- Customer Profile: Jobs, Pains, Gains
- Value Map: Products/Services, Pain Relievers, Gain Creators
- **Fit:** When pain relievers match pains, gain creators match gains

### Slide 8 — Coherence Check
> Do all 9 blocks tell ONE story?

**Check Your BMC:**
- Channels match segments? (Not LinkedIn for students)
- Costs align with revenue? (Not $1 price with $10 costs)
- Partnerships support activities?

## Part 4: Pitching (30 min)

### Slide 10 — 10-Slide Pitch Structure
| # | Slide | Time |
|---|-------|------|
| 1 | Title + Hook | 30 sec |
| 2 | Problem | 45 sec |
| 3 | Solution | 45 sec |
| 4 | Market | 30 sec |
| 5 | Business Model | 45 sec |
| 6 | Traction | 45 sec |
| 7 | Competition | 30 sec |
| 8 | Team | 30 sec |
| 9 | Financials | 30 sec |
| 10 | The Ask | 30 sec |

### Slide 11 — Storytelling: Before/After/Bridge
- **Before:** Current pain
- **After:** Desired future
- **Bridge:** How YOUR venture gets there

### Slide 12 — Handling Q&A
| Bad | Good |
|-----|------|
| Defensive | Curious |
| Bluff | Honest |
| Ramble | Concise |

**Key:** "I don't know" + "Here's how we'll find out" > bad answer

### Slide 13 — Elevator Pitch Formula
> "For [target], who [problem], our [solution] provides [benefit]. Unlike [competitor], we [advantage]."
