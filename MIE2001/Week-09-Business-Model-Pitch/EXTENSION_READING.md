# Week 9 — Extension Reading & Enrichment

## Core Reading (Required)

**"Business Model Generation"** — Alexander Osterwalder & Yves Pigneur
- The definitive BMC book
- Read the chapter on your specific block type

**"The Art of the Start"** — Guy Kawasaki (Chapter on Pitching)
- The 10/20/30 rule
- Practical pitch deck advice

## Enrichment Readings

### Business Model Canvas
1. **"Value Proposition Design"** — Osterwalder et al.
   - Companion to Business Model Generation
   - Deep dive on VPC with exercises

2. **"The Lean Startup"** — Eric Ries (Ch 9-11)
   - How BMC connects to Build-Measure-Learn
   - Pivot vs. persevere decisions

3. **"Running Lean"** — Ash Maurya
   - Lean Canvas (simplified BMC variant)
   - Practical for early-stage startups

### Pitching & Storytelling
4. **"Presentation Zen"** — Garr Reynolds
   - Design principles for presentations
   - How to create slides that communicate, not distract

5. **"Talk Like TED"** — Carmine Gallo
   - 9 public speaking secrets from TED talks
   - Storytelling techniques that work

6. **"Made to Stick"** — Chip & Dan Heath
   - Why some ideas survive and others die
   - SUCCESs framework: Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Stories

7. **Pitch Deck Examples** — Check "Pitch Deck Hunt" on Twitter/X or SlideShare for real startup decks

### Singapore Context
8. **"Singapore's Most Successful Pitch Decks"** — Search case studies on SG startups
   - Look for Carousell, Grab, or Foodpanda early pitch materials
   - Understand how Singapore-specific elements were positioned
