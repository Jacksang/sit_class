# Week 9 — Lab & Unit Test Design

## Lab: BMC Gallery Walk + Pitch Practice (50 min)

### Objective
Finalize your BMC AND get feedback on your pitch deck in one session.

### Part A: BMC Gallery Walk Setup (10 min)
- Pin/display your BMC on wall or screen
- Prepare a 2-min walkthrough
- Identify 1-2 specific areas where you need feedback

### Part B: Gallery Walk Rounds (20 min)
**Round 1 (10 min):** Half the teams present, half visit
**Round 2 (10 min):** Switch

**Visitors:** Leave sticky notes on each BMC:
- Green: something that works well
- Yellow: a question
- Red: something inconsistent

### Part C: Iteration (10 min)
Review feedback. Discuss with your team:
- What will you incorporate?
- What do you disagree with? Why?
- Update your BMC now.

### Part D: Pitch Practice (10 min)
**In groups of 3 (not your team):**
- 2 min pitch
- 2 min feedback
- Rotate

**Focus:** One specific improvement area: is your story clear?
