# Week 9 → Week 10 — Open Questions & Briefing Prep

## Briefing for Final Week
**Final submission:** Take-home Business Plan (due end of Week 10 — no class session)

**What to deliver:**
1. Final Business Plan (max 6 pages — see deliverable spec)
2. Final pitch deck (PDF, 10 slides max)
3. Peer evaluation (confidential, online form)
4. Final reflection: "My entrepreneurial journey" (500 words)

---

## Open Questions for Discussion

### Conceptual Questions
1. **BMC simplicity:** Is it possible to have a BMC that's "too simple"? What's the minimum viable BMC?

2. **Pitch deck design:** How much does visual design matter vs. the content? Is a polished Canva deck worth the time?

3. **The "Ask":** For a student startup, what's the most valuable thing you could ask for — funding, mentorship, connections, or something else?

### Application Questions
4. **Traction threshold:** If your MVP didn't get the results you hoped for, how do you present that honestly in a pitch? Is "we learned what doesn't work" valuable traction?

5. **Your BMC coherence check:** Read your BMC left to right. Does it tell one story? Where is the weakest link?

6. **Final reflection:** What's the single most important thing you've learned in this module? Has your view of entrepreneurship changed since Week 1?

### Singapore Context Questions
7. Singapore has a strong safety net and stable economy. Does this encourage or discourage student entrepreneurship?

8. Looking at the Singapore startup ecosystem, which BMC block do local founders typically excel at? Which do they struggle with?

9. Would you start a venture (even part-time) after this module? Why or why not?
