# MIE2001 — Topic Breakdown (10-Week)

---

## Topic 1: Entrepreneurship Mindset (Week 1)

**LO:** LO1 intro

**Lecture:** What is entrepreneurship? Myths vs. reality. Growth mindset (Dweck). Intro to effectuation vs. causation.

**Activity:** "Pitch a Bad Idea" icebreaker

**Deliverable:** Reflection — "My relationship with entrepreneurship"

---

## Topic 2: Effectuation Principles (Week 2) ⭐ Interview Demo

**LO:** LO1 core

**Lecture:** Sarasvathy's 5 principles (Bird-in-Hand, Affordable Loss, Crazy Quilt, Lemonade, Pilot-in-the-Plane). Causation vs. effectuation. Singapore cases.

**Activity:** "My Effectual Profile" worksheet + pair share

**Deliverable:** Reflection — apply one principle to a personal decision

---

## Topic 3: Design Thinking (Week 3)

**LO:** LO2

**Lecture:** Stanford d.school 5-stage model. Design thinking for business.

**Activity:** Mini design sprint — solve campus problem (60 min)

**Deliverable:** 3 HMW statements. Team formation deadline.

---

## Topic 4: Opportunity Recognition & Customer Discovery (Week 4)

**LO:** LO3

**Lecture:** Sources of opportunity. Problem-first approach. The Mom Test — interview techniques.

**Activity:** Campus scavenger hunt (30 min) + interview prep

**Deliverable:** Interview guide

---

## Topic 5: Opportunity Assessment & Market Sizing (Week 5)

**LO:** LO3

**Lecture:** Market sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM). Competitive analysis. Feasibility framework.

**Activity:** Market sizing workshop

**Deliverable:** **Customer Discovery Report due**

---

## Topic 6: Lean Startup, MVP & Midterm Check (Week 6)

**LO:** LO2, LO3

**Lecture:** Build-Measure-Learn. MVP types. Riskiest assumption testing.

**Activity:** MVP Design Sprint. Informal 10-min team check-in.

**Deliverable:** MVP test plan

---

## Topic 7: Startup Team & Choosing Customers (Week 7)

**LO:** LO4

**Lecture (Part 1):** Co-founder dynamics, equity, team charter, ethics
**Lecture (Part 2):** Customer segmentation, personas, channel selection (SG channels)

**Activity:** Team charter negotiation + channel brainstorming

**Deliverable:** Signed team charter + customer persona

---

## Topic 8: Project Finance & Singapore Funding (Week 8)

**LO:** LO5

**Lecture:** Revenue models. Unit economics (CAC/LTV). Break-even. Singapore grants and funding.

**Activity:** Financial model workshop (Google Sheets template)

**Deliverable:** Financial model

---

## Topic 9: Business Model Canvas & Pitching (Week 9)

**LO:** LO4, LO5

**Lecture (Part 1):** All 9 BMC blocks. Value Proposition Canvas. Coherence check.
**Lecture (Part 2):** Pitch deck structure. Storytelling (Before/After/Bridge). Q&A handling.

**Activity:** BMC gallery walk + pitch practice with peer feedback

**Deliverable:** **BMC due** + pitch deck draft

---

## Topic 10: Final Business Plan (Week 10 — Take-home)

**LO:** LO1-LO5

**No class.** Written submission only.

**Deliverables:**
1. Executive Summary (1 page)
2. Problem & Customer Discovery (1-2 pages)
3. Solution & MVP Results (1 page)
4. BMC (revised, 1 page)
5. Financial Projections (1 page)
6. Appendix (interviews, data)
7. Peer evaluation (confidential)
8. Final reflection (500 words)
