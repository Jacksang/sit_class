## Part 2: Design Thinking (40 min)

### Slide 4 — What is Design Thinking?
- **Stanford d.school 5-Stage Model:**
  - Empathize → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test
- Human-centered approach to problem-solving
- For BDS: apply to *business* not just product design

### Slide 5 — Stage 1: Empathize
> "Understand your user's experience and motivation"
- Observe, engage, immerse
- Methods: interviews, shadowing, experience mapping
- Example: Airbnb founders stayed with hosts

### Slide 6 — Stage 2: Define
> "Frame the problem you're solving"
- From raw empathy data → clear problem statement
- Tool: "How Might We..." statements
  - "How might we help SIT students find affordable lunch near campus?"

### Slide 7 — Stage 3: Ideate
> "Generate as many solutions as possible"
- Quantity over quality initially
- Techniques: brainstorming, SCAMPER, worst possible idea
- Rule: No judgment during ideation

### Slide 8 — Stage 4: Prototype
> "Build to think"
- Low-fidelity: sketches, paper models, storyboards
- Key: A prototype is a question made physical

### Slide 9 — Stage 5: Test
> "Test with real users, not friends"
- Show, don't tell. Observe behavior, not opinions.
- Iterate: go back to earlier stages based on feedback

### Slide 10 — DT for Business vs. Product
| Aspect | For Products | For Business Models |
|--------|--------------|-------------------|
| Empathize | User experience | Customer pain points |
| Define | Feature requirements | Value proposition |
| Ideate | Solution features | Revenue models, channels |
| Prototype | Wireframes | BMC drafts, landing page |
| Test | Usability | Customer interviews, MVP |
