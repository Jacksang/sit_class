# Week 10 — No Lab / Unit Test

**There is no lab session this week.** Week 10 is the take-home Final Business Plan submission week.

Instead of a lab, use this time to:

1. **Review your team's business plan** for completeness and coherence
2. **Practice your pitch** (record on video and self-critique)
3. **Complete the final reflection** individually
4. **Submit the peer evaluation**

## Self-Check: Business Plan Completeness

Run through this checklist with your team:

- [ ] Executive Summary is punchy — does it make someone want to read more?
- [ ] Problem statement is specific and backed by interview quotes
- [ ] Customer persona is specific enough (name, demographics, behavior, pain)
- [ ] Solution is clear and linked to the problem
- [ ] MVP results are honest (even if they didn't work)
- [ ] BMC is fully fleshed out and coherent (all 9 blocks)
- [ ] TAM / SAM / SOM numbers are realistic and explained
- [ ] Revenue model is clear and matches customer willingness to pay
- [ ] Break-even analysis makes sense
- [ ] Team section shows complementary skills
- [ ] Pitch deck is 10 slides, mostly visuals, consistent

## Self-Check: Pitch Readiness

- [ ] I can deliver my entire pitch without notes
- [ ] I know the answer to the 5 hardest questions
- [ ] My part is under 2 minutes and hits key points
- [ ] I've practiced at least 3 times
- [ ] I've watched my video recording and improved at least one thing
