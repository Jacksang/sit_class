## Part 1: Final Business Plan Structure

### Deliverable: Final Business Plan (6 pages max)

Your plan should synthesize everything you've learned and built this semester. It is the capstone artifact of the module.

### Page 1: Executive Summary (approx. ⅓ page)
A one-paragraph summary of your entire venture. Someone should read this and understand your business.

**Structure:**
- The problem you solve
- Who you solve it for
- How you solve it
- How you make money
- Your traction to date
- What you need (funding, resources, partnership) to achieve your next milestone

### Pages 1-2: Problem & Customer Discovery
**Contents:**
- Problem statement with evidence from customer interviews
- "How Might We" framing
- Key interview insights (include quotes)
- Your customer persona (name, demographics, behavior, pain points)

**Expected length:** 1-1.5 pages

### Page 3: Solution & MVP Results
**Contents:**
- Your value proposition
- Product/service description (simple — no technical specs)
- MVP test results
- What you learned from testing
- Iterations made based on feedback

**Expected length:** 0.5-1 page

### Page 4: Business Model Canvas (Revised)
**Contents:**
- Full BMC (visual or table format)
- Coherence check: does the model make sense?
- Value Proposition Canvas (VPC) mapping

**Expected length:** 1 page (BMC visual) + key insights paragraph

### Page 5: Financial Projections
**Contents:**
- TAM / SAM / SOM
- Revenue model + pricing
- Unit economics (CAC, LTV, LTV/CAC ratio)
- Break-even analysis
- Funding needed (if applicable)

**Expected length:** 1 page

### Page 6: Appendix
**Contents (as needed, not counted in 6-page limit if minimal):**
- Interview notes (raw)
- Competitor matrix
- MVP mockups/screenshots
- Team charter
- Team bios

## Part 2: Final Pitch Deck (10 Slides, PDF)

Submit alongside your Business Plan. Each slide should be self-explanatory.

| Slide | Content |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | Title + Tagline (one sentence) |
| 2 | The Problem (specific, emotional, with evidence) |
| 3 | Your Solution (show, don't tell) |
| 4 | Market Size (TAM → SAM → SOM visual) |
| 5 | Traction (interviews, MVP results, any revenue) |
| 6 | Business Model (revenue, pricing, unit economics) |
| 7 | Competition (matrix + your unique advantage) |
| 8 | Team (why YOU can do this) |
| 9 | Financials (projection + break-even) |
| 10 | The Ask (what you need + what you'll achieve) |

**Design tips:**
- 30+ pt font minimum
- More visuals than text
- Consistent color scheme
- One message per slide

## Part 3: Final Reflection (500 words)

Write individually (not as a team). Reflect on your entrepreneurial journey this semester.

**Prompt questions (answer at least 3):**

1. **Before vs. After:** How has your understanding of entrepreneurship changed since Week 1?

2. **Biggest surprise:** What was the most unexpected thing you learned — about your venture, your customers, your team, or yourself?

3. **Failure moment:** When did something not go as planned? What did you learn from it?

4. **Growth:** What skill or mindset did you develop that you didn't have before?

5. **Future:** Will you continue this venture? Start a new one? What would you do differently?

6. **Advice:** What advice would you give to a student taking this module next semester?
