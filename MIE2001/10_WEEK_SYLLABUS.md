# MIE2001 — 10-Week Syllabus (Final)

*Compressed from the original 13-week curriculum for first-year SIT Business Design School students.*

---

## Overview

| Aspect | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Duration | 10 weeks (compressed from 13) |
| Target | First-year BDS, SIT |
| Prerequisites | None |

### Key Compression Decisions

| What | Decision | Rationale |
|------|----------|-----------|
| Mid-term pitch | **Week 5** — full 5-min graded pitch | Preserved as important milestone |
| Final presentations | **Take-home Business Plan** (Week 10) | Reduces in-class pressure |
| Effectuation + Mindset | **Separate weeks** (W1 mindset, W2 effectuation) | Effectuation needs full session depth |
| Design Thinking + Opp Rec | **Separate weeks** (W3 DT, W4 Opp Rec) | Each needs active outdoor exercise |
| BMC + Pitching | **Combined** (Week 9) | Natural pair — pitch includes BMC |
| Team + Customers | **Combined** (Week 7) | Both are team-facing topics |
| No homework around project weeks | W5 (mid-term) and W10 (finals) | Students need space to present |

---

## Week-by-Week

| Week | Topic | Key Activity | Deliverable |
|------|-------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | Entrepreneurship Mindset | "Pitch a Bad Idea" icebreaker | Reflection |
| 2 | **Effectuation Principles** ⭐ Interview Demo | "My Effectual Profile" worksheet | Reflection |
| 3 | Design Thinking & Problem-Finding | Mini design sprint / campus walk | 3 HMW statements, Team formed |
| 4 | Opportunity Recognition & Customer Discovery | Campus scavenger hunt + interview prep | Interview guide |
| 5 | Opportunity Assessment & Market Sizing | Market sizing workshop | Customer Discovery Report **due** |
| 6 | Lean Startup, MVP & Midterm Check | MVP Design Sprint + informal pitch | MVP test plan |
| 7 | Startup Team & Choosing Customers | Team charter + channel brainstorming | Signed team charter + persona |
| 8 | Revenue Models, Unit Economics & SG Funding | Financial model workshop | Financial model |
| 9 | Business Model Canvas & Pitching | BMC gallery walk + pitch practice | BMC due + pitch deck draft |
| 10 | **Final Business Plan** (Take-home) | No class — final submissions | Business Plan + Peer eval |

---

## Assessment Structure

| Component | Weight | Due |
|-----------|--------|-----|
| Weekly Reflections | 20% | Weeks 1-9 |
| Customer Discovery Report | 25% | Week 5 |
| Business Model Canvas | 25% | Week 9 |
| Final Business Plan | 30% | Week 10 |
