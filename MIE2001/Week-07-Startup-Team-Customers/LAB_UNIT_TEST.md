# Week 7 — Lab & Unit Test Design

## Lab: Team Charter + Customer Persona (50 min)

### Objective
Finalize your team charter AND create a customer persona — two essential artifacts for the rest of the module.

### Part A: Team Charter Finalization (20 min)
**In your team, complete the charter together (from PPT).**

**Key areas to discuss:**
- Roles: Who does what? Is there clear ownership?
- Communication: Telegram/WhatsApp? Meeting frequency?
- Missed deadlines: What's the process? Escalation?
- **Hardest conversation:** What happens if someone stops contributing?

**Sign-off:** Each member signs (digitally or physically). Submit a photo/PDF.

### Part B: Customer Segmentation Worksheet (15 min)
**In teams:**

1. List all possible customer segments for your venture
2. For each, rate: Pain (1-5), Size (estimate), Accessibility (1-5)
3. Choose your PRIMARY segment

**Check:** Can you describe your primary segment in one sentence? "We serve [specific description] who need [specific need]."

### Part C: Customer Persona Creation (15 min)
**Using your actual interview data:**

1. Give your persona a name, age, background
2. Describe their daily routine
3. When and how do they experience the problem?
4. Write one quote in their voice (real or synthesized from interviews)

**Deliverable:** 1-page persona + channel strategy (submit by end of class)
