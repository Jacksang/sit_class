# Week 7 — Extension Reading & Enrichment

## Core Reading (Required)

**"Traction: How Any Startup Can Achieve Explosive Customer Growth"** — Gabriel Weinberg & Justin Mares
- Chapter on the Bullseye Framework
- 19 channels explained with case studies

## Enrichment Readings

### Startup Team & Co-Founders
1. **"The Founder's Dilemmas"** — Noam Wasserman (book, Ch 1-3)
   - The classic study of co-founder dynamics
   - Data on equity splits, founder exits, and team conflicts
   - Why "friends don't make the best co-founders" (sometimes)

2. **"Slicing Pie"** — Mike Moyer (book)
   - Dynamic equity splitting model
   - Practical tool for student teams with varying commitment levels

3. **"How to Split Equity with Co-Founders"** — Y Combinator (video, 10 min)
   - Practical advice from YC partners
   - Includes vesting and cliff mechanics

### Customer Segmentation & Channels
4. **"Crossing the Chasm"** — Geoffrey Moore (book, Ch 1-3)
   - The classic on technology adoption lifecycle
   - Why the early adopter → mainstream gap kills startups

5. **"The Mom Test" (Revisited)** — Rob Fitzpatrick
   - Chapters on identifying your real customer
   - How to segment based on interview data

6. **"Blue Ocean Strategy: How to Create Uncontested Market Space"** — Kim & Mauborgne
   - Strategy canvas and value innovation
   - Singapore cases included

### Singapore-Specific
7. **"Singapore Startup Ecosystem Report"** — SGInnovate / NUS Enterprise
   - Latest data on funding, sectors, and trends
   - Understanding "what's hot" in SG right now

8. **"SIT Innovation & Entrepreneurship Office"** — Check their website for current programs
   - Mentorship, grants, and incubation opportunities for SIT teams
