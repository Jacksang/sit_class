## Open Questions for Discussion

### Conceptual Questions
1. **Equity anxiety:** Is it fair for the person with the idea to get more equity? Or is execution more important?

2. **Team conflict:** What's the best way to handle a teammate who isn't contributing? Before the charter vs. after?

3. **Segmentation vs. inclusion:** Does segmenting your market exclude people who might benefit from your product? Is that OK?

4. **The Chasm:** Why do you think so many startups fail crossing from early adopters to mainstream? Is it a marketing problem, a product problem, or both?

### Application Questions
5. **Channel cost vs. effort:** In Singapore, organic channels (Telegram, word-of-mouth) are cheap but slow. Paid channels (ads) are fast but expensive. Which approach fits a student startup better?

6. **Early adopter identification:** Who are your early adopters? Are they the same people who would be your long-term customers, or different?

7. **Partnerships as channels:** Are you considering partnerships (with student clubs, SIT offices, companies) as a channel? How would you approach them?

### Singapore Context Questions
8. Singapore is a small market (5.6M). Does this affect how you think about segmentation? Is a very narrow segment viable in Singapore?

9. Singaporeans are efficient and prize convenience. How does this affect your channel strategy? (e.g., do Telegram/WhatsApp broadcasts feel intrusive or helpful?)

10. Team dynamics in Singapore context: International students + local students. Does cultural diversity help or complicate team dynamics?
